Global Parameters

Turns a stretch of responses into sound. Each step is one hour (within a chosen day) or one day (within a chosen range); whichever quadrant dominates a step picks a scale, how energetic/pleasant those responses were shapes the note, and how many there were shapes loudness. Uses the sidebar filters (location, energy, pleasantness, etc.) as the underlying data. Switch Scale palette to "Quadrant grid" to instead follow a single quadrant's own 25 buttons: each step's pitch is set by exactly where its responses sit on that quadrant's own 5×5 grid (row = how intense the energy, column = how intense the pleasantness/unpleasantness), spanning two octaves plus one note, and note length shortens as intensity rises for an audible tempo cue. Collapse this section with its triangle once it's set up, to keep it out of the way of the tracks below.

–

Each quadrant gets its own track: a channel strip on the left and its own lane on the right showing when that quadrant is sounding. Click a strip's triangle to collapse its controls (and shrink its lane) out of the way. Octave shifts each quadrant's notes up or down from wherever they're already pitched — 0 is no shift (roughly C4). Pan sets each quadrant's fixed left/right position. Reverb send controls how much of that quadrant reaches the shared reverb (100% = the master Reverb Amount above, 0% = none) — it only does anything once Reverb is switched on. Noise blends in a hiss under each quadrant's tone, from Off (pure tone) up to fully noise-dominant, useful for marking a quadrant as "noisier" or more chaotic-sounding. Glide is portamento — instead of jumping straight to a note's pitch, it slides up or down from the previous note that same quadrant played, from Off (instant, the old default) up to a slow, singing slide between consecutive notes; the Tails-across-gaps toggle above controls whether that glide survives a rest.

Drum Parameters

Below the quadrant tracks sit four more: an approximate, synthesized kick/hi-hat/snare, plus a 4th accent track that's either a Clap or a Clave — a toggle under its own triangle switches between the two — that loops under the reel. At 1x, each hour/day step in the timeline is one 16th-note of the pattern — so 16 timeline steps make one measure, and the backbeat's pulse is exactly the reel's own step tempo, speeding up or slowing down automatically with Tempo and Playback speed. Speed (in the bar above the drum tracks) doubles or halves that ratio (2x packs two 16th-notes into one timeline step; ½x stretches one 16th-note across two, for a halftime feel) without changing the reel's own tempo. Click a cell in a drum lane to arm that drum on that step; any combination, or none. Each track's own Fill/Rand/Clear (under its triangle) act on that row alone; Fill All/Randomize All/Clear All act on all four at once.

Exclude suspicious entries

When enabled, entries that appear to be spam, pranks, or accidental taps are excluded from all charts and counts.

Detection rules

  • 🚀 Speed — Response selected in under 2 seconds (faster than a person can meaningfully read and choose)
  • 🔁 Repeat — Same emotion chosen 3 or more times in a row within a single session
  • ⚡ Burst — 5 or more responses submitted within 60 seconds in a single session

Flagged entries are never deleted — turn this filter off to include them again.
