Screen 3 timing

The thank-you message, logged-feeling readout, the intro line, and the Health & Wellness Center contact card are fixed content, not editable here — see the RESOURCE_* constants near the top of this file to change any of that wording, the phone numbers, hours, or room number.

Inactivity check

On the emotion-grid screens (the quadrant views and "See all 100 emotions"), a "still there?" prompt appears after this many minutes with no taps. It gives a fixed 30-second countdown — if nobody responds, the kiosk returns to the home screen without logging anything.

Restart

Sends the kiosk back to Screen 1 immediately, without recording anything.

Locking down Escape

This page ignores Escape on its own screens, but no web page can stop the browser's built-in Escape-exits-fullscreen shortcut — that's intentional on every major browser. For real lockdown, launch Chrome in its own kiosk mode from a shortcut instead of clicking Full screen here:

chrome.exe --kiosk "http://localhost:8000/kiosk.html"
